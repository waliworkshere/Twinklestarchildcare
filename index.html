About TwinkleStar

A second home where every child is seen, safe and celebrated.

TwinkleStar International ChildCare is a nursery in Al Hail North, Seeb, caring for babies, toddlers and pre-schoolers in a healthy and secure environment where parents can confidently entrust their children.

  • Values and morals, taught simply. Our management and staff are trained to teach good values in a simple, professional way.
  • Sensory-based learning. Children explore, touch, build and create, learning through all their senses.
  • Bilingual from the start. Everyday English and Arabic help children grow confident in both languages.
  • Activities for every child. Customised activities match each child's needs and developmental level.
“The first nursery in the region awarded by the American Board for Best Nursery Management.”TwinkleStar International ChildCare
